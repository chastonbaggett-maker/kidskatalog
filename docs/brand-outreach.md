# Brand outreach (templates only)

Cold copy for toy brands. **Do not send from this repo.** Pair with the offer in [`docs/brand-deals.md`](./brand-deals.md): video permission, a featured Watch card, and a parent-only CTA on `/p/deals`. Kids never see Buy.

Replace `[Brand]`, `[Toy]`, and `[Your name]`.

---

## Cold email

**Subject:** Featured video card + parent CTA for [Toy] (not Amazon)

Hi [Name] —

KidsKatalog is a kid catalog (no buy buttons for kids). Parents open a separate Parent Mode page.

We would like permission to feature a short [Toy] video on Watch, plus a parent-only partner card on `/p/deals`.

What we need:

1. Written video permission (or a brand-supplied clip)
2. A parent landing URL that is **not** Amazon (your store, retailer, or campaign)
3. Partner name for the parent disclosure
4. OK that we may earn a commission on that parent click

Kids stay commerce-free. Amazon Buy stays on a different Parent Mode control — never the same click as your card.

Happy to send a one-pager or a draft card. Thanks for considering it.

[Your name]  
KidsKatalog — kidskatalog.vercel.app/p/deals

## Follow-up (if they reply “send more”)

Point them at `docs/brand-deals.md` and `docs/brand-affiliates.md`: fields (`brandAffiliate` `{ partner, network, url, live }`, plus legacy `brandDeal` / `brandDealUrl` / `brandPartner`), FTC split (partner disclosure ≠ Amazon Associates), dual-claim (separate clicks only), and that we do not put Amazon Product Advertising Content on an off-Amazon card.
